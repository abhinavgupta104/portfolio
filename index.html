01 Agent · personal

Stella

My own AI agent. She lives on a server, talks to me on WhatsApp and by voice, and drives my laptop when there is work to do.

Give her a project and she becomes the tech lead. She plans it, hands the tasks to a team of other AIs, reviews their code, runs the tests and tells me honestly what is still broken.

  • 12AIs she can brief at the same time
  • 81/81tests passing on the CRM her team built
  • 9rooms in her dashboard, from memory to voice

Docker · WhatsApp · Gemini Live · Chrome DevTools Protocol · SSH

02 Agent · voice

Kavya

A voice agent that makes and answers real phone calls, in Hindi and English, through an ordinary Android phone and its SIM.

There is no telephony provider in the loop. The laptop takes over the phone's calls over Bluetooth. She listens, thinks and speaks in one model, hangs up by herself and writes a summary after every call. Ask her if she is a robot and she tells the truth.

  • 1.25sto answer you mid-conversation
  • <1sfrom pickup to her first word
  • 2languages, on one SIM card

Pipecat · Gemini Live · PipeWire telephony · Bluetooth HFP · Python

A prototype. She only calls numbers that agreed to test, which is what India's telecom rules ask for.

03 Agent · marketing · for Nucleus Ai

NACC

The marketing agent I built for Nucleus Ai. Ask it on WhatsApp or from its dashboard and it makes the post, cuts the video or sets up the ad campaign.

It works from each brand's kit (logo, colours, voice), knows which festival is next week, and reviews its own work before anything goes out.

  • 4kinds of output: posts, videos, ads, websites
  • 90sto build a website from one sentence
  • 2ad platforms it runs: Meta and Google

Gemini · WhatsApp · React dashboard · Meta & Google Ads APIs

04 Agent · lead finder

LeadScout

Type “gyms in Noida”. It sweeps Google Maps, Justdial and business directories, then opens each business's own website to pull the phone, email and socials.

Stella's AI dev team wrote the app around it. Big requests are split area by area, de-duplicated, and come back as a clean spreadsheet.

  • 35real leads in 26 seconds, for “cafe in Noida”
  • 100gyms found in 2 minutes 40 seconds
  • 500leads in a single request

Node · headless Chrome · SQLite · React

05 Platform · component library

Tatva

तत्त्व means element. Tatva/ui is my React component library: 91 animated components you install as source, and a composer that assembles them into a full page.

Describe a page in one sentence and it builds it. My agents use the same parts to hand a local shop a finished website in about a minute and a half.

  • 91components in 11 categories
  • 3,600+automated checks on the library
  • 1command to install, also from AI assistants over MCP

React · CSS · shadcn registry · MCP · Vite

06 Platform · messaging · for Nucleus Ai

SMS Portal

A complete bulk-SMS platform: a client portal and an admin console on top of the open-source Jasmin gateway, connected to a real carrier route and delivering to real phones.

It enforces India's DLT rules (entity IDs, sender headers, template matching) before a message is allowed out, then tracks every delivery receipt.

  • 25pages across the client and admin sides
  • 4sfrom pressing send to “delivered”
  • 195automated tests, 35 of them in a real browser

FastAPI · React · Jasmin · RabbitMQ · Redis · Docker · Caddy

07 Platform · chat · for Nucleus Ai

WhatsApp Panel

The WhatsApp Business panel Nucleus Ai's customers log in to: chatbot flows, an AI agent, broadcasts and a shared inbox. It stands on the Wapto codebase. I run it and extend it.

My biggest addition is payments inside the chat. A business connects its own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal. A flow block sends the link, waits, then branches on paid or not paid and tells the owner.

  • 3payment gateways a business can plug in
  • 10items a customer can choose from in one message
  • 0unsigned webhooks accepted

Node · MongoDB · React · WhatsApp Cloud API · Razorpay · Stripe · PayPal
